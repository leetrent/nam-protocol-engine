Project Snapshot: NAM Protocol Engine
1. Project Overview & Architecture
Goal: Build and verify a "NAM Protocol Engine" to automate regulatory compliance and 3Rs justification for non-animal testing methods (OECD Test Guidelines).

Core Stack: Python 3.12, uv, pytest, Pydantic v2 schemas, Gradio UI, BM25 text retrieval (GuidelineIndex).

Key Modules:

src/schemas.py: Pydantic models enforcing structured regulatory protocols, guidelines, and citations.

src/extractor.py & src/search.py: PDF section chunking parser and BM25 guideline retrieval index.

src/matcher.py: Rules-based protocol matcher mapping queries to specific OECD NAM alternatives.

src/strategy.py: Tiered testing strategy planner and quantitative decision models (e.g., PIF/MPE for phototoxicity, peptide depletion for DPRA).

src/app.py: Gradio frontend user interface containing the Guided Strategy, Search Workbench, and Quantitative Decision Calculators.

2. Integrated OECD Protocols
OECD TG 431: RhE skin corrosion (sub-categorisation 1A vs 1B/1C).

OECD TG 432: In vitro 3T3 NRU phototoxicity (PIF, MPE, and MEC screening criteria).

OECD TG 437: BCOP corneal opacity and permeability (severe eye damage, IVIS > 55).

OECD TG 439: RhE skin irritation (50% cell viability threshold).

OECD TG 442C: In chemico skin sensitisation / DPRA (Key Event 1 molecular initiating event).

OECD TG 492: RhCE reconstructed human cornea-like epithelium (eye irritation).

OECD TG 497: Defined Approaches for skin sensitisation (2-out-of-3 and Integrated Testing Strategy).
